Two of the most culturally significant manga series of the 2010s each hit their tenth anniversary this week and both treated the milestone as an occasion to actually deliver something. Between them: an unexpected music tie-up, a streaming platform doing damage control on its own price hike, and a publisher that helped define Western manga trying to build the next generation of readers.

![My Hero Academia 10th anniversary key visual — promotional image for the "More" special episode (©Kōhei Horikoshi/Shūeisha, Bones)](https://static.animecorner.me/2025/12/1766106044-5ba974b69237e29dae851cbb53462ea2-768x432.png)

## My Hero Academia Turns Ten with a Timeskip Special and a World Concert Tour

The *My Hero Academia* anime's 10th anniversary arrived with a concrete deliverable: ["More"](https://animecorner.me/my-hero-academia-anime-sequel-timeskip-special-episode-in-may-2026-adapting-chapter-431-10th-anniversary-announcements/), a standalone episode adapting chapter 431 — the post-ending chapter from the manga's final collected volume — premiered May 2 exclusively on Crunchyroll worldwide (excluding Asian territories) and on Nippon TV/Yomiuri TV in Japan. The episode picks up eight years after graduation, making it the furthest the anime has gone into the future of the story. Alongside it, [Crunchyroll announced a free streaming marathon of the full series](https://www.cbr.com/mha-free-streaming-final-anime-release/) and a global concert tour, *My Hero Academia in Concert*, which opens May 30 at Pacifico Yokohama before expanding internationally. Whether the concert tour is a commercial celebration or a staging ground for a future film or timeskip sequel announcement is the only remaining question of substance; Bones and Shūeisha have said nothing beyond the anniversary packaging itself.

## The Promised Neverland Marks Ten Years with New Material and a Stage Musical

On May 5, Shūeisha [announced a full package of 10th anniversary projects for *The Promised Neverland*](https://animecorner.me/the-promised-neverland-announces-new-one-shot-for-summer-2026-more-major-projects/): a new one-shot from creators Kaiu Shirai and Posuka Demizu scheduled for *Weekly Shōnen Jump* this summer, a full-scale stage musical, a full-color digital manga release, a pop-up store, and an online lottery campaign launching in July. Preview pages from the one-shot place the story in 2050, with Emma, Ray, and Norman now in their late twenties and living in peaceful coexistence — a direct continuation of the "good ending" the manga's final chapter gesturally promised but didn't show. The more significant story here is Demizu agreeing to return at all; she has been publicly candid about the pain of the editorial decisions that compressed the manga's final arc, and her participation in anniversary material represents some degree of resolution with the project.

## Hikaru Utada Releases "PAPPAPARADISE" — First New Music in a Year

Hikaru Utada's ["PAPPAPARADISE"](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/press-release/2026-05-06/hikaru-utada-releases-new-single-pappaparadise-today-official-music-video-now-available-on-youtube/.237112) dropped May 6, their first new release since 2025, serving as the 14th ending theme for the long-running children's anime *Chibi Maruko-chan*. The music video, directed by Tomokazu Yamada, casts Utada as a taxi driver collecting a series of passengers — a different register entirely from their usual output. A 7-inch vinyl edition follows June 24. The tie-up carries some biographical weight: [Utada has said](https://www.bandwagon.asia/articles/hikaru-utada-enters-pappaparadise-with-whimsical-new-chibi-maruko-chan-theme-watch) that *Chibi Maruko-chan* was the series that connected them to everyday Japanese life after returning to Tokyo from New York as a nine-year-old. That's not a standard commercial pitch — it makes the choice of franchise feel genuinely personal rather than managed. The song is also doing double duty as the commercial song for the tea brand Ayataka.

## Crunchyroll Launches Ani-May Discount Two Months After Raising Prices

[Crunchyroll began its Ani-May promotional deal on May 7](https://animecorner.me/crunchyroll-launches-premium-membership-sale-for-ani-may/): three months of the Fan tier for $1.99/month, or Mega Fan for $2.99/month, running May 7–21 across more than 35 countries. The context matters: in February 2026, Crunchyroll raised Fan from $7.99 to $9.99 and Mega Fan from $11.99 to $13.99. Running a promotion at roughly 80% off standard pricing two months after a 25% price increase is a fairly direct signal that the February hike produced subscriber churn significant enough to require a recovery window. Whether subscribers who return at $1.99 stay at $9.99 in August is the real question — Crunchyroll won't publish those figures, but the magnitude of the Ani-May discount is itself evidence about how the hike landed.

## Seiyuu Tomoyo Kurosawa Announces Birth of First Child

On May 1, [Tomoyo Kurosawa announced the birth of her first child](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/interest/2026-05-01/voice-actor-tomoyo-kurosawa-announces-birth-of-1st-child/.237002). Kurosawa is currently voicing Miki Hosokawa in the Spring 2026 revival of *Hell Teacher: Jigoku Sensei Nube*, and has Selma Therese in the upcoming *Puella Magi Madoka Magica -Walpurgisnacht: Rising-* film (August 28) on her schedule. Her longer credit list includes Cocoa in *Is the Order a Rabbit?*, Izumi Miyamura in *Horimiya*, and Elfnein across the *Symphogear* run. She married in June 2023. In her statement, she said she is "cherishing every moment of motherhood and will continue to dedicate myself to my work as a voice actor."

## Tokyopop Launches a Children's Publishing Imprint

[Tokyopop announced TOKYOPOP Kids on May 5](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-05-05/tokyopop-launches-new-tokyopop-kids-imprint/.237105), a new imprint targeting readers 12 and under, with first titles in fall 2026. The initial lineup of seven titles spans manga, graphic novels, picture books, chapter books, and two Spanish-language releases. Tokyopop was one of the publishers that established manga distribution in Western markets in the late 1990s and early 2000s; shifting into original content for young readers rather than licensed catalog work is a meaningful pivot. The Spanish-language inclusions in the launch slate are the detail worth watching — they either signal a genuine commitment to non-English readership or they're two tokens in a press release. Tokyopop's influence on the American manga market was built on distribution through bookstores and mass-market retail channels; if TOKYOPOP Kids secures placement in school library systems or Scholastic fairs, that's a real pipeline. If it's shelf space at the same specialty retailers the adult catalog already reaches, it's just a rebrand.

## Trails Series Gets Multiple Figure Announcements at Fan Meeting

At the [Trails in the Sky the 1st Fan Meeting held in Japan on May 2](https://operationrainfall.com/2026/05/02/multiple-trails-series-figure-prototypes-unveiled/), Kotobukiya and Good Smile Company both unveiled figure prototypes from Nihon Falcom's long-running *Trails* RPG series. Kotobukiya showed a decoration master of Alisa Reinford from *Trails of Cold Steel III–IV*, and their 1/12 scale Iris kit — first revealed in October — opened for pre-order with a May 2026 ship date. Good Smile Company's contribution is a Pop Up Parade Large of Renne from *Trails in the Sky SC*, using her younger design from her first appearance in that game. The choice of Renne's *SC* design over later appearances is a deliberate signal aimed at series veterans; GSC could have used any number of Renne's appearances across the franchise, and the decision to anchor her to the moment she first landed with the fanbase is exactly the kind of choice that distinguishes collector-oriented announcements from mass-market ones.

## Seven Seas Licenses Two Light Novels

Seven Seas added two titles to its Airship LN imprint on May 7: [*Villager A Wants to Save the Villainess No Matter What!*](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/press-release/2026-05-07/seven-seas-licenses-villager-a-wants-to-save-the-villainess-no-matter-what-light-novel-series/.237145) by Kōtarō Isshiki and [*A Serious Error in Chihaya-chan's Reputation*](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/press-release/2026-05-07/seven-seas-licenses-a-serious-error-in-chihaya-chan-reputation-light-novel-series/.237146) by Hisumi, both early-stage acquisitions without English release dates. The volume counts and genres are exactly what Seven Seas' Airship catalog looks like at this stage of the acquisition pipeline; no further details are available yet.
