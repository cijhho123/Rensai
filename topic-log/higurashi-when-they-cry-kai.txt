2026-04-29: character-analysis - Argued that Higurashi Kai is not primarily a mystery-resolution show but a psychological recovery narrative: Frederica Bernkastel is the entity formed when Rika's accumulated 100 years of dying made hope structurally unavailable, and Kai's central argument is that rationality alone cannot restore hope — only direct emotional investment in specific people (Keiichi's refusal to accept the loop's evidence) can. 2026-05-24: villain-analysis - Argued that Kai's real thesis is about moral accountability and the impossibility of transferring sin to others. Miyo Takano's "Herostratic Fame" motivation (mass murder as path to eternal infamy) is the show's sharpest idea; she is defeated not by heroism but by being made to live unremembered, while the Old Maid card-game metaphor makes the structural argument explicit: no sacrifice needed, just restore the missing piece. Draws the parallel between the 2007 broadcast cancellation (media blaming Kai for a real murder) and the show's own thesis about externalizing blame.
